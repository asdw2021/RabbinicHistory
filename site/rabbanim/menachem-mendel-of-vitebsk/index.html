ר׳ מנחם מנדל מוויטבסק

Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk
1730–1788
Known as: ר׳ מנדלי ויטבסקער
Follow this story on the map

ר׳ מנחם מנדל מוויטבסק (c.1730–1788), also associated with Horodok, was a leading disciple of ר׳ דוב בער המגיד ממזריטש and one of the principal organizers of early Hasidism in Belorussia and Lithuania. After the Maggid’s death he emerged as a senior leader of the movement. In 1772 he and ר׳ שניאור זלמן מליאדי attempted to meet ר׳ אליהו מווילנה in order to reduce the escalating conflict between Hasidim and their opponents, but the meeting did not occur. Persecution subsequently displaced his circle from Minsk. In 1777 he led an extraordinary migration of roughly three hundred Hasidim to Ottoman Palestine, accompanied by ר׳ אברהם מקאליסק. The group first settled in Safed and later transferred its centre to Tiberias, creating a durable Hasidic presence in the Land of Israel while maintaining financial and spiritual connections with Eastern Europe through letters. His teachings and correspondence were collected posthumously as פרי הארץ. The record distinguishes his documented relocations from later devotional traditions and uses no unauthenticated portrait.
