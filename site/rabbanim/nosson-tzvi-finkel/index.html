ר׳ נתן צבי פינקל (הסבא מסלבודקה)

Nosson Tzvi Finkel
1849–1927
Known as: הסבא מסלבודקה
Follow this story on the map

ר׳ נתן צבי פינקל (1849–1927), universally known as the Alter or Saba of Slabodka, was a formative architect of the Lithuanian Musar yeshiva. Born in Raseiniai and orphaned young, he grew up among relatives and entered the circle of ר׳ שמחה זיסל זיו, the Alter of Kelm, who shaped his educational outlook. In Kovno and its Slabodka suburb he organized kollelim and, during the early 1880s, established Knesses Yisrael. Its distinctive ideal of גדלות האדם—cultivating the dignity and latent greatness of the human personality—joined rigorous Talmud study to disciplined ethical formation. He recruited and mentored scholars rather than normally delivering the principal Talmud lecture himself; among the institution’s leaders and alumni were ר׳ משה מרדכי אפשטיין, ר׳ איסר זלמן מלצר, ר׳ אהרן קוטלר and ר׳ יחזקאל סרנא. He also helped create or strengthen branches and associated institutions at Slutsk and elsewhere. During the First World War the yeshiva was displaced into the Russian interior before returning to Lithuania. When Lithuanian educational regulations threatened its traditional curriculum, he supported transferring a major branch to Mandatory Palestine. He joined the students in Hebron in 1925 and guided the new Knesses Yisrael there until his death in Jerusalem. His talks and letters were assembled posthumously as אור הצפון and related collections, transmitting the Slabodka approach internationally.
