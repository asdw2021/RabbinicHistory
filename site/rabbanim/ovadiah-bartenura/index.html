ר׳ עובדיה מברטנורא

Ovadiah Bartenura
1445–1515
Known as: הרע״ב
Follow this story on the map

ר׳ עובדיה ירא מברטנורא (בערך 1445–1515), commonly called the Bartenura or רע״ב, was an Italian rabbi, traveler and the author of the standard commentary on the Mishnah. A student of ר׳ יוסף קולון, he served communities in Bertinoro and Città di Castello before leaving Italy in 1486. His surviving letters describe a journey through Rome, Naples, Salerno, Palermo, Rhodes, Alexandria, Cairo, Gaza, Hebron and Bethlehem, ending in Jerusalem shortly before Passover 1488. Finding a small and impoverished community, he became its rabbinic and communal leader, strengthened charitable provision and Torah study, and established a yeshiva. His concise Mishnah commentary, drawing especially on ר׳ שלמה יצחקי and ר׳ משה בן מימון, became the most widely printed traditional guide to the text.
