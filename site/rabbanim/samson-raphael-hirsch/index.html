ר׳ שמשון רפאל הירש

Samson Raphael Hirsch
1808–1888
Follow this story on the map

ר׳ שמשון רפאל הירש (1808–1888) was a German rabbi, biblical commentator and principal architect of modern Neo-Orthodoxy. Born in Hamburg, he studied with חכם ר׳ יצחק ברנייס and in Mannheim under ר׳ יעקב יוקב עטלינגר before attending the University of Bonn. He served as Landesrabbiner of Oldenburg, district rabbi in Emden, and chief rabbi of Moravia and Austrian Silesia in Nikolsburg. In 1851 he accepted leadership of Frankfurt’s newly formed Israelitische Religionsgesellschaft, building schools, welfare institutions and an independent Orthodox communal structure. His formula Torah im Derech Eretz argued for uncompromising halakhic life together with disciplined engagement in general culture and civic society. Through The Nineteen Letters, Horeb, his German Pentateuch commentary and the journal Jeschurun, he articulated Judaism as an integrated system of law, symbolism and ethical vocation. He opposed Reform and fought for legal permission for Orthodox Jews to secede from religious communities they regarded as incompatible with their commitments.
