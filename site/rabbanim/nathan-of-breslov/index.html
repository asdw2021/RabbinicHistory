ר׳ נתן שטרנהרץ מברסלב

Nathan of Breslov
1780–1844
Follow this story on the map

ר׳ נתן בן ר׳ נפתלי הרץ שטרנהרץ (1780–1844), called ר׳ נתן מנמירוב, ר׳ נתן מברסלב and מוהרנ״ת, was the principal disciple, scribe, editor and disseminator of ר׳ נחמן מברסלב. Raised in a prosperous, learned and initially non-Hasidic Nemyriv family, he studied Talmud and halakhah intensively and married into the family of ר׳ דוד צבי אוירבך. A spiritual search brought him to ר׳ נחמן in Bratslav in 1802. He began recording the master's discourses with exceptional care, helped prepare ליקוטי מוהר״ן, and after ר׳ נחמן's death preserved conversations, biography and tales that otherwise would largely have been lost. He served as Breslov's informal leader but never claimed to be rebbe, maintaining ר׳ נחמן as the movement's sole master. He established the annual Rosh Hashanah gathering at Uman, traveled widely to strengthen small communities, undertook a hazardous pilgrimage to the Land of Israel in 1822, acquired a printing press and devoted his life to publication. His own major works—especially ליקוטי הלכות and ליקוטי תפילות—creatively apply Breslov teaching to halakhah, prayer and daily religious life rather than merely transcribing his teacher. During the anti-Breslov campaign led by ר׳ משה צבי מסאווראן, he endured denunciation, arrest, restrictions and temporary exile from Bratslav. He returned, rebuilt the weakened community and died in Bratslav in 1844. Because much detailed chronology derives from his sacred autobiography and internal Breslov sources, this record distinguishes firsthand testimony, later communal memory and independently documented publication history.
