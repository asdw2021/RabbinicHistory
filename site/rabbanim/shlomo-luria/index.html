ר׳ שלמה בן יחיאל לוריא

Shlomo Luria
1510–1573
Known as: מהרש״ל
Follow this story on the map

ר׳ שלמה בן יחיאל לוריא (1510–1573), known as the מהרש״ל or רש״ל, was a leading sixteenth-century Ashkenazic halakhist and Talmudist. Born according to the prevailing tradition in Brest-Litovsk, he spent part of his youth with learned relatives in Poznań and studied in Lublin under ר׳ שלום שכנא. He was also associated with the Ostroh scholarly circle and married Lipka, daughter of ר׳ קלונימוס הברקסטן. After rabbinic service in Brest and other Lithuanian communities, he became rabbi and rosh yeshiva in Ostroh around 1550 and moved to Lublin in the mid-1550s. There he created an independent academy and became one of Polish Jewry’s most authoritative decisors. His ים של שלמה reconstructs practical halakhah through close examination of the Talmud, earlier commentators and communal custom; it often resists the compression of law into codes. חכמת שלמה records critical corrections and explanations based on manuscripts and early printed Talmuds, while his responsa illuminate ritual, civil and communal law. He corresponded and disagreed sharply but respectfully with his relative and former fellow student ר׳ משה איסרליש. Later authorities prized both his textual independence and insistence that legal conclusions remain grounded in the sugya. He died in Lublin on 7 November 1573 and was buried in its Old Jewish Cemetery.
